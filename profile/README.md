# Independent Society of Knowledge  

Welcome to the **Independent Society of Knowledge**, a global initiative dedicated to advancing knowledge, research, and education. ISK serves as a platform where experts, researchers, and innovators collaborate to push the boundaries of human understanding.  

This repository represents the central hub of ISK, housing foundational resources, organizational documentation, and strategic initiatives.  

## **Our Mission**  
We believe that knowledge should be **unrestricted, collaborative, and transformative**. ISK fosters an environment where science, education, and technology converge to create meaningful impact.  

## **Repositories and Projects**  
ISK Portal oversees projects that define and guide our organization, including:  
- **Strategic Documentation** – Vision, governance, and policy documents.  
- **Design Assets** – Branding, UI/UX guidelines, and creative resources.  
- **Cross-Department Initiatives** – Collaborative projects between ISK’s research, education, and development divisions.
